PALOMA BAY
A SUN-SOAKED CRIME STORY
Theo Marchetti drove rally cars. Now he drives the night taxi — and owes the Vireo family forty thousand. Eight missions. Three choices. Three endings.

HOW TO PLAY

Drive: WASD to drive, SPACE handbrake, SHIFT nitro (with the upgrade), E enter / exit car, H horn.
On foot: WASD to move, mouse to aim (click to lock the mouse), LEFT CLICK shoot, RIGHT CLICK aim, 1–5 or wheel to switch weapons, R reload.
World: E interacts — start missions, buy guns, upgrade the car, save at the safehouse. M opens the map. Z calls Mara for a free repair. X lets Zed fence your car for cash. N changes radio station.
Police: stars rise when you are seen committing crimes. Break line of sight to lose them — or respray at Mara's Garage to clear your wanted level.
Choices: three story choices move your LOYALTY and FAVOR. They decide your ending: Clean Getaway, King of the Bay, or Burn It All.
